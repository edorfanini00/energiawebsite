A building that thinks ahead.

Energia AI is an AI control platform for commercial HVAC. It learns how each zone of your building heats and cools, reads weather forecasts and occupancy schedules, and adjusts setpoints before rooms drift. Every decision weighs energy against comfort.

Talk about your building

No two buildings hold heat the same way.

West glass takes the afternoon sun. A full floor on Tuesday runs warm by eleven. Fixed schedules treat every day the same and correct a room only after it drifts. Energia AI learns how your building responds, then plans the next hours around it.

From model to setpoint.

  1. Model

    We build a thermal model of your building from its floor plans, HVAC equipment and operating history. Physics describes how heat moves between zones. A neural network learns the behavior physics misses.

  2. Learn

    A reinforcement learning agent trains inside that model across simulated weather and occupancy conditions. It is rewarded for holding comfort and penalized for wasted energy.

  3. Control

    On each control cycle the agent reads the building, checks the forecast and proposes new setpoints. Every proposal is checked against your limits before it reaches the building management system.

Operators set the limits.

Energia AI’s control architecture is designed around boundaries agreed with your facilities team. Every proposed setpoint is checked against them before it reaches your BMS.

Setpoint range
Heating and cooling setpoints stay inside the range you approve, with a deadband between them to avoid simultaneous heating and cooling.
Fresh air
Outdoor air minimums for your site, based on standards such as ASHRAE 62.1, are part of the control constraints. Ventilation is not traded for energy.
Equipment
Equipment limits are defined with your facilities team before control is enabled.
Fallback
If the connection drops or a reading looks wrong, control hands back to your existing sequences.
Life safety
Fire, smoke and critical equipment alarms take priority. Energia AI steps back and your building’s own systems and manual override take over.
Your BMS
We connect through open protocols such as BACnet and Modbus. Compatibility is confirmed with your team during an assessment.

Questions

Which buildings is Energia AI for?

Commercial buildings with central HVAC run through a building management system. An assessment tells us whether yours is a good candidate.

What data do you need?

Floor plans, HVAC equipment details, BMS points with trend history, and your occupancy schedules. Weather forecasts come from public sources.

How do you assess savings?

We compare a proposed control strategy with how the building runs today. Results depend on your equipment and the operating data your building keeps. Simulation results are not a guarantee of savings in your building.

Does it need hardware on site?

Expect a small gateway next to your BMS. It reads points and writes setpoints over your building’s protocols. The AI runs on that gateway or in our cloud, depending on your security requirements.

Can operators take control back?

Yes. Your existing control sequences stay in place underneath. Operators keep manual override, and if Energia AI loses its connection or sees bad data, control returns to those sequences.
