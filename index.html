How we work and how we deliver measurable savings.

How fast can Energia AI be deployed?

We move at the speed of your portfolio. Our platform connects to your existing BMS over BACnet, eliminating the months wasted in traditional retrofit cycles. One integration call activates our optimization engine in days, not quarters — ensuring your most critical buildings start saving immediately.

Does it work with my existing BMS?

Yes. Energia AI is vendor-neutral and speaks BACnet, Modbus, and Niagara natively. We layer intelligence on top of Johnson Controls, Siemens, Honeywell, Schneider, and Trane systems without replacing a single controller.

How much can we expect to save?

Commercial buildings typically see 15–30% HVAC energy reduction in the first year, plus peak-demand savings. Every kWh is measured against an IPMVP-aligned baseline, so savings are verified — not estimated.

How does Energia differ from a traditional BMS upgrade?

A BMS executes fixed schedules; Energia thinks. We forecast load, learn your building's thermal behavior, and continuously write optimal commands back — a closed loop that gets smarter every day, with no new hardware on the wall.
