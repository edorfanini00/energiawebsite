Privacy

This page explains what happens to the details you send us through the form on the Energia AI website, and what the site measures when you visit.

What we collect

The answers you give in the form: your name, work email, company, building type and total floor area, and any optional details you choose to add, such as your role, number of buildings, building management system, annual energy spend, what you would like to improve and a phone number.

We also record when you sent the form, the page you sent it from and any campaign tags (UTM parameters) in the link you followed to reach us.

How we use it

We use these details only to reply to you about your building. We do not sell them, share them for marketing or add you to a mailing list.

Where it is kept and who can see it

Your answers are stored in a private database run for us by our hosting providers, Vercel and Prisma. Only the Energia AI team can read them, through a password protected dashboard.

Spam protection

To keep automated messages out, your browser solves a small calculation before the form is sent. To limit repeated submissions we keep a keyed hash of your IP address and email address. These security records expire within one day and are then deleted automatically.

Meta pixel

This website uses the Meta pixel, a measurement tool from Meta Platforms, to learn whether our ads on Facebook and Instagram bring people to the site and lead to inquiries. When you visit, your browser loads the pixel from Meta and tells Meta which page you viewed and, if your browser shares it, the page you came from, such as a Meta ad. Like any request, this also gives Meta your IP address and browser type. The pixel may set cookies named _fbp and _fbc, which let Meta connect your visit to an ad you clicked.

When you send the form, the pixel tells Meta that an inquiry was sent, with its reference number. It does not send your answers.

The site uses no other analytics, advertising tools or third party scripts, and sets no other cookies for visitors. You can block or delete these cookies in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries until we no longer need them or you ask us to delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you or delete the details you sent at any time. To request deletion, email edo.orfanini@energiaigroup.com.
